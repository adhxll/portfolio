Hi, nice to e-meet you! I have worked in iOS development for 5+ years now. I love being technical and being able to bridge the understanding of technical terms to non-technical people. In fact, I am pretty good at it, if I do say so myself. Communication as a software engineer has always been a strong skill in my professional career. Reviews from my peers always highlight my aptitude in thorough documentation and clear communication with stakeholders. But of course, I don't just communicate nothing. The content came from my technical expertise as well.
I love love love coding & digging deep into why things worked. I did study computer science for my bachelor's degree, but beyond that I always seek new things to learn. Aside from iOS development, I also had some background in NLP research when I was still studying. So in my free time, I did dive a bit into ML Ops, ethical AI, and also writing (check my Substack). Learning & growth have always been my passion, which is why I became a technical mentor in an international academy. Strengthening my mentorship & pedagogy skills has helped me to not only propel my learners, but also myself.
Other than my professionally related interests, I also love reading & playing guitar. I usually read two books at once, fiction and nonfiction. I really enjoy reading light novels, fantasy, contemporary romance, science fiction, and pop science. My favourite book is The Demon Haunted World by Carl Sagan. A lot of my philosophy and thinking process are influenced by the books that I have read throughout the years. If I have to describe my thinking in one sentence, it would be open-minded but sceptical.
You can probably tell already, I am not much of an extrovert. I always jokingly said to my friends that I am a domesticated human and I like my concrete jungle. Please don't ask me to go on a hike or a run during the weekends, I will probably fake a mysterious illness that will be gone after that weekend. Instead, I usually spend my weekends recharging at home. I journal & write, I play guitar, or learn something that I am currently interested in. I also love trying out new coffee shops because I love coffee & desserts (esp. strawberry shortcakes). Also, don't worry about my seeming lack of physical activity. I do cardio & (sort of) callisthenics at home.
